# Welcome to Canadian Ocean Modelling (COM) 🌊

Welcome to the Canadian Ocean Modelling GitHub, which is part of the efforts of the Canadian NEMO Ocean Modelling Forum Community of Practice (NEMO CoP). The goals of this forum are to encourage collaboration across Canadian institutions to share our NEMO modelling progress, development, code, accomplishments, successes and failures, and many other aspects. This is one of the Communities of Practice that MEOPAR supports. Click here to see the official website of the CoP: https://canadian-nemo-ocean-modelling-forum-community-of-practice.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html

## Main goal

- To make the NEMO modelling community in Canada more efficient and collaborative by sharing technical issues and solutions; this helps other groups in Canada avoid spending a large amount of time solving the same issue
- Help facilitate discussions around common, yet not necessarily publishable coding work and problem-solving done by different groups in Canada

## Community rules
1. Be respectful
2. Avoid jargon and complicated phrasing (not everyone has English as a first language, and people don't have the same background, which makes this community so vibrant and interesting!) -- basically try to make the information as accessible as possible
3. Spell out acronyms
4. Where possible, provide names of individuals (with their permission) of work done to allow for discussion and contact during discussion
5. Do not use this platform for any private discussions!

## How to contribute
