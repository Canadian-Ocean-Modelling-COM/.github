# Welcome to Canadian Ocean Modelling (COM) 🌊

Welcome to the Canadian Ocean Modelling gitHub which is part of the efforts of the Canadian NEMO Ocean modelling forum Community of Practice. The goals of this forum is to encourage collaboration across Canadian institutions to share our NEMO modelling progress, development, code, accomplishments, successes and failures, and many other aspects. This is one of the Communities of Practice that MEOPAR supports. Click here to see the official website of the CoP: https://canadian-nemo-ocean-modelling-forum-community-of-practice.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html
